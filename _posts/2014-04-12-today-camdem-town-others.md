Hello readers,

yesterday we behaved as proper Spanish tourist and felt like them. We went to visit the most tourist market in London: Camdem town! There we have lunch after getting hardly crazy because there were lot of options after that we went for a "small" walk.

Then we started walking from Camdem market to Regent's park, from there to Hyde park then Green park and finally to St. James park. We were kind of exhausted but we really like it and again with a wonderful weather, there are the testimonies (by the way: are we in London, Venice? :O):
