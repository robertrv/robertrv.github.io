## Job search pages

After you have this more or less clear, you can start sending your CV or Resume to the main UK web pages that the recruiters uses to find candidates:

- [http://www.jobsite.co.uk](http://www.jobsite.co.uk) : One of the most useful, you have to look there!
- [http://www.reed.co.uk](http://www.reed.co.uk) : Very useful also.
- [http://my.monster.co.uk/](http://my.monster.co.uk/) : this is the local version of monster, the good point is that maybe you get also offers from other parts of the world
- [http://www.seek.com.au/uk/](http://www.seek.com.au/uk/) : Not very usefull

I would say that the most important think is to **update regularly your CV,** because then this CV appears better situated when the recruitment searches.

## Accommodation

Once or after we have a job or at least the way to find it, is time to search a place to live. London is a very dynamic place, it applies to everything also the accommodation, and with lot of possibilities. The hotels are all around London but probably is the most expensive option. I would just evaluate two possibilities: Hostels and Flat/House share or rent.

### Hostels

At this point there is also lot of possibilities like you can find in Internet, but to me the best option are Hostels in [LHA group](http://www.london-hostels.co.uk/).

### Flat/house share/rent

For this there are some specialized web pages:

- [http://www.spareroom.co.uk/](http://www.spareroom.co.uk/): Really good, also easy to search, and with lot of information for each flat
- [http://www.gumtree.com/](http://www.gumtree.com/): This is one of the most used web pages for this porpoise. The problem in this web page is that is not specialized to this, it implies that the information is not structured in the same way, but really useful.
- [http://www.findaproperty.co.uk](<http://www.findaproperty.co.uk >)
