[NexOpen](https://nexopen.dev.java.net/) is a new J2EE framework which complements other frameworks [Spring](http://www.springframework.org/), [Struts](http://struts.apache.org/), [jBPM](http://www.jboss.com/products/jbpm), [Hibernate](http://www.hibernate.org/), [Xfire](http://xfire.codehaus.org/), etc (some of them, like Spring which is the Core of NexOpen, are at the same time metaframeworks). <!--more--> This framework has the intention to facilitate repetitive and costly task of learning and configuration with the already extended J2EE frameworks, to let developers concentrate on the important business. To achieve this goal NexOpen gives some Wizards, Eclipse plugins.

It is suppose to be not just a tool to develop, also the quality of this development using desgin patters, JSR and being easy to use with tools like [Maven2](http://maven.apache.org/), [Continuum](http://maven.apache.org/continuum/), [jProfiler](http://www.ej-technologies.com/products/jprofiler/overview.html), [Jira](http://www.atlassian.com/software/jira/), [Confluence](http://www.atlassian.com/software/confluence/)...

[NexOpen](https://nexopen.dev.java.net/) has just been published, and you can see the [blog](http://bcurtu.blogspot.com/) where will be explained and it's [web page](https://nexopen.dev.java.net/). I know about this from one of the authors of the "baby", Bosco Curtu, who is also the author of the blog. I am really happy to see that his work (and his mates work) finally has a [visible](http://bcurtu.blogspot.com/2007/06/nexopen-goes-public.html) result! He and his mates has been working for long time in their company where they have developed this new framework.
